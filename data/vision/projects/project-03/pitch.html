01 The big idea

A single home for every play space — and everyone who runs one.

Parents and players find the right spot on a live map. Venue owners fill their calendars without phone calls. Cities keep equipment safe and compliant. All of it talks to web, mobile, and an AI assistant from day one.

/ BOOK

/ PLAY

5

Remind

BullMQ jobs: 24h & 1h before

No double-bookings, ever. Every confirmation runs inside a database transaction with a SELECT … FOR UPDATE row-level lock. The second concurrent attempt is cleanly rejected and any Stripe Payment Intent already created is auto-refunded — the WebSocket broadcast only fires after the commit.

10 AI-ready by design

Every action is an LLM-callable tool.

The OpenAPI spec at /api/docs-json is the machine-readable manifest. pgvector powers natural-language venue search.

GET/api/v1/venues/:id/availability
check_availability — open slots for a venue on a date
→ venue_id, date, duration
POST/api/v1/bookings
book_slot — concurrency-safe booking + Stripe payment
→ venue_id, slot_id, customer_id
POST/api/v1/search/semantic
search_venues — natural language → pgvector ranking
→ query, location, filters
GET/api/v1/venues/:id
get_venue_details — full profile, equipment & schedules
→ venue_id, locale
PATCH/api/v1/bookings/:id/cancel
cancel_booking — cancels & triggers Stripe refund
→ booking_id
POST/api/v1/offers
request_custom_offer — opens a negotiation thread
→ venue_id, customer_id, message
🧠

pgvector semantic search. Embeddings for descriptions, rules, amenity tags & equipment are generated async via BullMQ. Ask in plain language: “shaded playground with a sandbox near me, good for toddlers.”
