01 The big idea

A single home for every play space — and everyone who runs one.

Parents and players find the right spot on a live map. Venue owners fill their calendars without phone calls. Cities keep equipment safe and compliant. All of it talks to web, mobile, and an AI assistant from day one.

/ FIND

Discover instantly

Filter by type, amenities, price, distance, rating & “open now”. Map markers glow green, amber or red with live capacity.

/ BOOK

/ PLAY

03 Three ways to book

Pick the model that fits the venue.

Each venue runs exactly one booking mode — shown clearly on its profile. All times fixed to Europe/Berlin.

MODE 01

Time-Slot

Auto-generated bookable slots inside the seller’s operating hours. Pay now, slot locks live.

  • 1 Pick a slot
  • 2 Pay via Stripe
  • 3 Slot locked in real time + reminders scheduled
MODE 02

Period Rental

Separate daily & weekly rates. Pick a start date and period — the calendar blocks instantly.

  • 1 Choose dates
  • 2 Daily or weekly
  • 3 Pay & period blocks on the calendar
MODE 03

Custom Offer

A private chat thread for non-standard deals. Negotiate, then the seller sends a formal offer.

  • 1 Request & chat
  • 2 Seller sends a formal offer
  • 3 Accept → pay in thread → confirmed
↳ Anatomy of a confirmed booking
1

Select

Customer picks a slot or period

2

Promo

Optional code validated via Stripe

3

Pay

Stripe Payment Element

4

Lock

Row-level DB lock — exactly one wins

5

Confirm

Email + push, calendar blocked

6

Remind

BullMQ jobs: 24h & 1h before

No double-bookings, ever. Every confirmation runs inside a database transaction with a row-level lock (or a unique active-slot constraint). The second concurrent attempt is cleanly rejected and any Stripe payment auto-refunded — the WebSocket broadcast only updates screens after the commit.

04 What's inside

Built for real operations — not just bookings.

A connected toolkit that keeps venues safe, full, and discoverable.

Live map & smart filters

Type, amenities, price, distance, rating, “open now”, safety-certified — markers coloured by live capacity.

Live capacity

🟢 🟡 🔴 traffic-light headcount, broadcast in real time.

QR defect reports

Scan at the gate → report a broken swing in seconds, no login.

Verified reviews

Only after a completed booking. Sellers reply once, publicly.

Notification center

Real-time bell + Expo push to mobile, grouped by day.

Promo codes

Stripe-powered discounts, scoped per venue.

Compliance & safety

Certificates, expiry reminders & a public “Safety Certified” badge.

Maintenance workflow

Tickets route to nearby Service users within range → accept/decline live → permanent maintenance report archived to the venue. 4-hour no-response escalation to the seller.
